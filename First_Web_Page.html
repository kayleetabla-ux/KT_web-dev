Dramas

Asian dramas are amazing. The romance plots are very fun to watch. They vary greatly from slow burns to dramatic and tragic. The deep meanig and tear jerking plots are great especially for when its late at night. Magical plots with nine tailed foxes and demons that could possibly be the male lead are there for when your bored and want a little angst. Slow burns are there for when you're along for the ride, when you want a long term romance show that will last you awhile. A good remendation for a K-drama is Queen of Tears. Its full of drama and tear filling scenes. A C-drama that is great for slow burn is Hidden love. another great sweet romance that is so fun to rewatch.

    • Anime

    Anime, much like asian dramas, are great when they're romance. However the action magic filled plots are wonderful. They draw you into the movements af the fights and the art and colors bring so much life to the story. Solo Leveling is a great example of this. The animation is beautiful and the fight scenes really intruiging. Different animation styles also bring the plot to life. More cutesy, curved lined, water colored art style is perfect for slow burn romance plots. More hard edge lines are great for action filled stories
